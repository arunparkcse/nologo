Our Story

Born From A
Love Of Stories

Many years ago, a bunch of youngsters, bitten by the travel bug and curious about the world, set out on a journey.

Along the way, they met like-minded people — clients, collaborators and team members across sectors and geographies — who shared their belief that good communication begins with good thinking.

Somewhere along that journey, nologo was born.

From brand identities and films to campaigns, social impact and digital content, we've continued to explore new ways of making communication relevant, distinctive and memorable.

18 years on, the journey continues…
