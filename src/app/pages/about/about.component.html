The Beginning

Born From A
Love Of Stories

It all began with an unquenchable thirst for travel. There is something absolutely magnetic in ever-stretching oneself into the unknown, breaking through boundaries and transcending barriers — evolution is inevitable.

For those who have tasted such liberation, there is no looking back. Anything that touches human life — nay, life with humanity — touches us. The world is abound with examples; the more we seek, the more we find.

Nologo is our unending journey in the world of communication where we constantly seek unchartered territories and explore creative means of sharing myriad stories of life and living.
